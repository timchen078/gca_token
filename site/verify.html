Anti-scam checklist

Stop when a request crosses these lines.

Official GCA support will not ask for wallet or exchange secrets.

  • Never share secrets

    Do not send private keys, seed phrases, wallet passwords, verification codes, or exchange API secrets.

  • Never enable withdrawals

    Do not grant exchange withdrawal permission or transfer assets to a support, verification, or unlock address.

  • Never trust artificial proof

    Paid callers, fake volume, self-trading, and guaranteed listings are not trust signals.
