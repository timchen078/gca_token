Operating principles

Clear by design.

GCA separates verifiable facts, user-controlled software, and manually reviewed services.

Fixed supply

The token contract has no post-deployment mint function.

User control

GCA does not hold user wallets or request withdrawal access.

Transparent tools

Browser tools show their inputs and calculations and do not place orders.

Public accountability

The founder, contract, market route, support address, and project documents are published.

Leadership

Founder-led and publicly accountable.
