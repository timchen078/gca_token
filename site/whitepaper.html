Executive summary

Narrative meets product. Product prioritizes risk control.

GCA turns the Go China Access narrative into a product system that people can use and independently verify. Users can read market research, use browser-based risk tools, create a member account, and confirm token and market identity through official pages.

GCA AI Quant Access supports research, planning, position sizing, risk checks, backtesting, and review. The public tools do not custody wallets, request withdrawal permission, or automatically execute trades for users.

Token design

Fixed supply. One verifiable identity.

GCA is an ERC-20 token on Base Mainnet. The full supply was created at deployment. The contract has no post-deployment mint function, owner or admin role, proxy upgrade path, blacklist, pause function, transfer tax, or project withdrawal path.

Membership

Eligibility, records, and benefits are reviewed separately.

The membership system uses read-only public wallet verification and manual review. It never asks for a private key, seed phrase, wallet password, or withdrawal permission.

Operating scope

The public product focuses on research and risk control.

The public product provides 10 browser-based research and risk tools, member accounts, read-only wallet verification, eligible ledger records, and manually reviewed services.

Connected exchange market data and automated trading modules are not part of the current public product. Any future trading connection must first pass privacy, reliability, risk-control, simulation, or testnet checks.
