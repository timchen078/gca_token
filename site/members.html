Safety Boundary

Verification without custody.

The official Member Access page accepts only the published account and review fields. It never requests private keys, seed phrases, wallet approvals, signatures, custody, exchange API secrets, or withdrawal permission.

Credits and membership records do not create trading permission or bypass product risk controls.

Member Utility

Credits and membership records.

Every benefit follows the published account, verification, and review process. No benefit is a cash balance or automatic token claim.

100 Credit Rules

The current holder benefit is a one-time account-level service credit record for verified 10,000 GCA holders. Credits support reports, backtests, risk warnings, ENTRY_READY signal review, position calculators, and education access.

Member Rules

The current GCA Member review requires a complete observed 30-day holding history and a verified current balance of at least 1,000,000 GCA. The 10,000 GCA member benefit remains a separate manual reserve-wallet review after approval.

No cash, income, reimbursement, trading permission, or risk-control bypass is created by credits or membership status.

Support Workflow

One official path for account support.

Use support@gcagochina.com for account review, missing evidence, wallet verification, or device-access recovery. Support can clarify the process but cannot override eligibility, verification, or safety rules.
