Published boundaries

Know what GCA does and does not control.

These boundaries apply across the token, browser tools, member account, and support workflow.

Fixed supply

1,000,000,000 GCA were created at deployment. The contract has no subsequent mint function.

User-controlled assets

GCA does not hold user funds or request private keys, seed phrases, or withdrawal permission.

Read-only verification

Member balance checks use public Base data and do not require a wallet signature.

Independent review status

No third-party audit has been completed. Liquidity is not represented as locked.

On-chain identity

Match every address before interacting.

The official route is GCA/USDT on Base Mainnet. Any different chain, token contract, pool, or quote asset is not the published GCA market identity.
